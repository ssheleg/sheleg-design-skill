A tinted pill on `--surface-3` whose INK carries the state — `bl-chip--good`,
`bl-chip--warn`, `bl-chip--accent`. The fill stays neutral because the brand and
the status hues are 3.18:1 and below: they may tint a surface and they may not be
the thing you read.

```tsx
<Chip>Queued</Chip>
<Chip tone="accent">Beta</Chip>
```
