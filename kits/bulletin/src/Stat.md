A figure on the bare paper, with its label above it and its source below. The
value is the display face at `--t-section`; the label is `--ink-soft` at
`--t-sm`. No card, no offset — a stat that is boxed competes with the cards
around it, and the pack already spends its elevation on those.

`source` is not decoration: a number with no stated origin is the thing this
library's evidence rule exists to refuse.

```tsx
<Stat value="17,000+" label="Businesses and agencies" source="Sept 2026" />
```
