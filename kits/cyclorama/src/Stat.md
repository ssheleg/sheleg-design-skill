The figure at `--t-metric` (up to 104px) in the **display** face, its label
above in mono at `--ink-soft`, the source below.

Setting the figure in the monospaced display face is not a stylistic tic: every
glyph in that face is one advance wide, so a column of metrics lines up without
`tabular-nums` and without a second numeric font. It is the one place this
pack's typographic constraint pays a direct dividend.

Give `source` whenever the number came from somewhere a reader could check. A
figure with no source on a page selling transformation is the exact claim a
sceptical executive discounts first.

```tsx
<Stat value="30" label="Days of diagnostic" source="per engagement, 2026 cohort" />
```
