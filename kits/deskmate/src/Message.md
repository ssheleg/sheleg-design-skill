One turn inside the frame. `person` and `app` are the only kinds, because the whole
argument of a page in this pack is that two of them alternate — and `app` is the one
that carries the `APP` badge, drawn in the client's own badge colours rather than in
the brand's.

A message never takes a brand colour. If it needs to say a state, it says it in words
and takes the glyph beside it: status here is never by colour alone.

```tsx
<Message author="Agent" kind="app" time="10:20 AM">
  Reconciled attendance and collections.
</Message>
```
