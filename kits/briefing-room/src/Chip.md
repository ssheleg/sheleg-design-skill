The pack's stated escape from bullets: pill radius, `--panel-2` fill, hairline
border, mono uppercase at 10.5px. Use a chip rail wherever a slide would
otherwise grow a list — markets, integrations, stages, segments. `tone="accent"`
marks the single item the slide is actually about, and `selected` is the same
fill behind a full accent border for the one already chosen. Chips are labels,
not controls: nothing here is clickable, and a chip that wants an action is a
`Button`.

```tsx
<Chip>UK</Chip>
<Chip>EU</Chip>
<Chip tone="accent">US — live Q3</Chip>
```
