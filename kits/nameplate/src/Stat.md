A figure with its label above it and, where the number came from someone else,
`source` beneath. On this pack `source` is not decoration: the page's whole argument
is borrowed proof, and an unattributed number is the one thing that breaks it.

Value in `--ink` at the section size, label in `--ink-body`, `source` in
`--ink-soft` at 12px.

```tsx
<Stat value="185M+" label="Monthly readers" source="Publisher-reported reach" />
```
