**The grid keeps its rhythm through an absence.** On the reference's content
calendar an empty day still renders its bordered cell — the month's shape is the
information, and collapsing the empty cells would destroy it to save space nobody
asked for.

So this component is a bordered box that states what is not there, and carries at
most one action. It is not a full-page illustration.

```tsx
<EmptyState title="No articles scheduled this week" />
```
