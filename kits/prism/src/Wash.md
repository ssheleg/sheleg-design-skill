One static gradient — `--wash-1` through `--wash-4` at 100deg — across the first
viewport, ending in a hard edge.

**Spent once, at the top.** A second gradient anywhere in the page is the
fastest way to turn this pack into a generic AI landing page.

**Never animated.** The stops are deliberately light: `--ink` sits above 18:1
over all four, so the headline needs no scrim, and deepening them means adding
one you should not need.

```tsx
<Wash><Heading level={1}>The High-Performance Vector Database</Heading></Wash>
```
