Mono throughout, numbers right-aligned, a `1px --line` bottom rule.

Right alignment is not decoration: it is the only arrangement in which a column
of figures can be compared without reading each one. This pack argues from
measurements, and the benchmark table is where the argument actually happens.

```tsx
<BenchmarkRow label="p99 latency" baseline="120 ms" value="14 ms" />
```
