The pack's loading idiom, and it is **static**. Blocks at radius 6 filled at 6%
of the ink, sized to the element they stand in for — same heights, same widths,
so nothing jumps when the data lands.

No spinner and no shimmer. The reference's skeletons do not animate, and a
shimmer is motion spent telling the reader something they already know.

```tsx
<Skeleton width="38%" />
<Skeleton width="72%" />
<Skeleton height={64} />
```
