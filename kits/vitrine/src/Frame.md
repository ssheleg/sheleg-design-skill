**The framed record** — a hairline rectangle with a 1px inset highlight, holding
a specimen of the thing the product manages. The inset line is the whole
difference between a case and a bordered box with a drop shadow, and removing it
turns the pack's signature into something every pack has.

`foot` is the honesty line and should almost always be given: a frame that shows
a specimen without saying it is one is a mock interface wearing evidence's
clothes.

```tsx
<Frame caption="What the workspace holds" foot="Drawn from the product's own routes">
  <RecordRows />
</Frame>
```
