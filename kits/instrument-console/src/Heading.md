The type ramp made visible, in the neutral grotesk this pack runs everywhere:
weight 600, clamp-scaled, leading tight but tracking never pulled negative.
`1` is the hero and stops at the pack's 5.25rem ceiling, `2` is a section, `3` a
panel title. Headings are `--ink` and carry no accent, no gradient and no
display or serif face — the section index that would normally decorate one is a
separate mono `ActBadge`, which is where the console voice actually lives.

```tsx
<Heading level={1}>One console for the whole fleet</Heading>
<Heading level={2}>Downlink control</Heading>
```
