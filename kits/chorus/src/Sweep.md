The three-stop gradient, measured: `--coral` → `--periwinkle` → `--parchment`. Hot to
cool to paper, and **never reversed** — the order is the pack's argument, that a hot
question cools into an answer on the page's own paper.

It is decoration and carries no text at any size. The periwinkle exists here and as a
series line on a slab; promoting it to a second button colour makes a different pack.

Gradients in this pack **do not move**. A shimmer or an animated sweep is banned.

```tsx
<Sweep />
<Sweep angle={271} height={4} />
```
