The closing band's scatter: stadium-radius pills at `--r-pill`, some outlined in
`--line-on-slab`, some filled `--coral`, `--periwinkle` or `--parchment`, jumbled at
angles between platform marks. Many voices, one page — it is the pack's name made
visible.

The tilt is a static transform, not an animation: nothing in this pack moves on
scroll. Keep it under about 12 degrees, which is the range the reference's own scatter
occupies.

Decorative by construction — a capsule carrying a word would be a coral or periwinkle
label, and neither may be text.

```tsx
<Capsule tone="coral" tilt={-6} />
<Capsule tone="outline" tilt={3} />
```
