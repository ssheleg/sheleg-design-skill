**A skeleton, never a spinner.** The corpus's only `@keyframes` is the vendor loading
spinner Framer ships, so there is no authored loader to measure, and inventing one
would be inventing a value.

`--line-quiet` bars at `--r-badge` in the shape of the content that is coming. They do
not pulse and they do not shimmer: a shimmer is a gradient in motion, and this pack's
gradients do not move. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there is nothing to
collapse, because there was never anything running.

```tsx
<Skeleton lines={4} />
```
