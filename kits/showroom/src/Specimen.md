One real product surface, framed, under `--shadow-specimen`. It is the whole
argument of this pack: the company's case is that the software is good, so the
page shows the software.

**One per page.** Two exhibits is a catalogue.

**Crop, never scale.** Below roughly 85% the chips stop being readable and the
type inside stops matching the type around it — the surface stops reading as
evidence and starts reading as a picture of evidence. Pass `height` and let the
viewport cut it.

Inside the frame, nothing casts. Contents are divided by `--line-weak`
hairlines, exactly as `workbench` divides them; the seven-layer stack lifts the
frame and nothing else. Mixing the two is what makes a screenshot look like a
collage.

```tsx
<Specimen title="Companies" height={620}>
  <ColumnHeader>Company</ColumnHeader>
  <DataRow selected>Vercel</DataRow>
</Specimen>
```
