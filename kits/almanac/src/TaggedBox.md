**The drawn box with its label notched through the top edge** — the reference's
own device, and the reason a 2px rectangle in this pack reads as a plate rather
than as a border.

`tag` is required by the type: a box without one looks unfinished, and the tag
is also what carries meaning where the seam cannot. It is uppercase mono and
never a sentence.

```tsx
<TaggedBox tag="Managed accounts">
  <AccountRows />
</TaggedBox>
```
