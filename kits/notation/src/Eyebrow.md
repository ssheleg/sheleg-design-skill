The uppercase mono label that names a section above its serif heading, tracked
`+0.08em`. It is the pack's only voice besides the serif and the sans, and it
**never says a sentence** — a name, a count, a unit, and nothing with a verb in
it.

```tsx
<Eyebrow>Specification</Eyebrow>
<Heading>What the layer holds</Heading>
```
