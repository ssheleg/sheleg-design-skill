One cream solid: `--model-top`, `--model-face`, `--model-side`, separated by
`--model-line` — the field colour — so the model reads as cut paper rather than
as an SVG with strokes.

**Axonometric, never perspective.** No vanishing point; every block keeps its
true proportion and the drawing stays measurable.

**Every block is labelled.** An unlabelled axonometric is decoration. If a block
has no name you can defend, remove the block.

**It does not move.** Parallax, rotation, exploded views and scroll-linked
assembly are all banned by the pack — an exploded view on scroll is the most
tempting and most damaging thing you can do here.
