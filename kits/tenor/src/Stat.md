Mono, `tabular-nums`, with the label above in the eyebrow step rather than below — the
reference reads label-then-value everywhere, because the label is what the reader is
scanning for.

A row of stats sizes against its **container**, so three across in a section become one
column in a sidebar without a viewport query.

```tsx
<Stat value="1,248" label="leads routed" source="last 30 days" />
```
