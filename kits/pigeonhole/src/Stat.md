A figure over its label, with an optional source line. The value takes the
display face at `--size-head` and **weight 400** — this pack never sets a number
in a heavier weight, which is what keeps a metric from reading as a boast.

```tsx
<Stat value="20,000+" label="Professionals" source="self-reported, 2026" />
```
