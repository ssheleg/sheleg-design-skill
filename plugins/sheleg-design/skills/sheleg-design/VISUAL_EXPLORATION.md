# Visual exploration — the invariant/open-axis split

The brand lock and the composition search are DIFFERENT locks, and treating
them as one is how a "preserve the brand" brief collapses into a single
direction nobody chose (VD-01). What a brand lock fixes, and what it leaves
open, is written down BEFORE any render:

| Layer | State | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Locked invariants** | fixed, every direction obeys them | brand colors, logo, accessibility floors |
| **Open axes** | explored, directions MUST differ on ≥2 | composition, type hierarchy, control anatomy, motion purpose |
| **Hypothesis** | one sentence per direction | what this direction believes about the task |

## How many directions

Proportional to the task, never a ritual:

- **an under-determined brief** («улучшить, сохранить бренд») — **two, at
  most three** small renders of ONE real key state, in one brand system,
  differing visibly on at least two open axes;
- **an already-explicit decision** («исправь spacing по точному Figma») —
  **one**, and exact-Figma reproduction generates NO variations at all;
- an EXISTING token file closes nothing by itself: tokens are the brand's
  half, composition is still open — refinement keeps identity while
  allowing a different rhythm inside the scope.

## The comparison, and the choice

Directions are compared on the SAME scenario, state and viewport — the
matrix from SKILL.md's pack comparison applies unchanged, one cast, no new
skill. The choice is made on arguments and on task preservation (does the
key state still do its job?), not on taste words: **neither a style pack,
nor dial numbers, nor the count of fonts is evidence of craft** — they
describe a direction, they do not grade it. Everything here runs OFFLINE:
paper-grade renders in the repo's own stack, no concept roll, no imagegen.

## After the choice

The chosen direction is PROVISIONAL until formalized: semantic tokens are
written (the `@adapter` contract), the deferred decisions are recorded by
name, and only a direction being published as a reusable pack owes the full
13-heading pack contract — a local concept identity is a legitimate,
lighter form for one product.
